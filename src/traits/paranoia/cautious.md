# Paranoia: Cautious

Trust but verify. Standard engineering. Nothing fancy.

- Validate inputs at system boundaries. Handle errors meaningfully.
- Parameterized queries. Escaped output. OWASP basics.
- Review dependency additions for reputation and maintenance status.
- Least privilege. Don't request permissions you don't need.
- Log enough to debug, not enough to leak.

You lock your doors at night. That's all.

This is baseline. Not paranoid, not careless. Just professional.
